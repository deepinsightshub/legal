# Data Deletion Instructions

**Application:** AI app (Meta App ID 26453566720910322)
**Operator:** Deep Insights Hub
**Contact:** deepinsightshub@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 2 October 2026

## What this application holds

The AI app is an internal advertising-analytics tool. It stores aggregated
advertising performance data — spend, impressions, clicks, conversion counts, and
the statistical age/gender/placement breakdowns that Meta supplies. It does not
store names, email addresses, phone numbers, profile content, messages, or any
other consumer personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy).

In most cases, therefore, there is no personal data about an individual to
delete.

## For a Business Portfolio administrator

To stop all collection and have stored data removed:

1. Open **business.facebook.com** → **Business settings**.
2. Go to **Business assets** → **Apps**, or **Users** → **System users**.
3. Select **AI app** and choose **Remove**.

Revoking access takes effect immediately. Stored access tokens are destroyed on
revocation, and the associated performance data is deleted within 30 days if you
request deletion at the address below. If you make no request, performance data
is retained for up to 24 months and then deleted automatically.

To have the data removed sooner, email **deepinsightshub@gmail.com** with the
subject **"Data deletion request"**, naming the Business Portfolio or ad account.

## For an individual

If you believe the application holds personal data concerning you, email
**deepinsightshub@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"** and
enough detail to locate it. You will receive:

- Acknowledgement within 7 days.
- A substantive answer within 30 days, confirming either that the data has been
  deleted or that no data concerning you was held.

No account is needed to make a request, and there is no charge.

## Revoking at the Meta level

You may also remove the application's access from your own Facebook settings at
**Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites**. This revokes the
permission directly with Meta and is independent of any request made here.
