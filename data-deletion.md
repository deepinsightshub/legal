# Data Deletion Instructions

**Application:** AI app (Meta App ID 26453566720910322) — the Meta integration
behind the xFalcon / PULSE marketing-intelligence platform.
**Operator:** Deep Insights Hub
**Contact:** deepinsightshub@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 2 October 2026

Our full privacy policy is at
[xfalcon.ai/privacy-policy](https://xfalcon.ai/privacy-policy) and our terms at
[xfalcon.ai/terms-of-service](https://xfalcon.ai/terms-of-service). This page
covers deletion specifically, as Meta requires.

## What the Meta integration holds

Through the Meta Marketing API and Instagram Graph API, and only for Business
Portfolios that have granted access, the integration reads aggregated
advertising performance data: campaign, ad set and ad names; spend, impressions,
clicks, reach and conversion counts; and the statistical age, gender, placement
and device breakdowns that Meta itself supplies.

It does **not** receive or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal
addresses, friend lists, messages, photos, profile content, or payment details
of any consumer. It sets no cookies and runs no tracking of its own. In most
cases there is therefore no personal data about an individual to delete.

## For a Business Portfolio administrator

To stop all collection and have stored data removed:

1. Open **business.facebook.com** → **Business settings**.
2. Go to **Business assets** → **Apps**, or **Users** → **System users**.
3. Select the app and choose **Remove**.

Revocation takes effect immediately and destroys the stored access token.
Associated performance data is deleted within 30 days of a request to the
address below; absent a request it is retained for up to 24 months to allow
year-on-year comparison, then deleted automatically.

## For an individual

If you believe we hold personal data concerning you, email
**deepinsightshub@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"** and
enough detail to locate it. You will receive acknowledgement within 7 days and a
substantive answer within 30 days, confirming either that the data has been
deleted or that none concerning you was held. No account is needed and there is
no charge.

## Revoking at the Meta level

You may also remove the application's access from your own Facebook settings at
**Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites**. This revokes permission
directly with Meta, independently of any request made here.
