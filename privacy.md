# Privacy Policy

**Application:** AI app (Meta App ID 26453566720910322)
**Operator:** Deep Insights Hub
**Contact:** deepinsightshub@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 2 October 2026

## What this application is

This is an internal marketing-analytics and advertising-management tool. It is
operated by Deep Insights Hub for its own business accounts and those of clients
who have explicitly authorised it. It is **not** a consumer product, it has no
public sign-up, and it does not offer Facebook Login to members of the public.

## Who can use it

Access is limited to named administrators of the Meta Business Portfolios that
have granted the application permission. There is no other route to use it.

## What data it accesses

Through the Meta Marketing API and Instagram Graph API, and only for Business
Portfolios that have granted access, the application reads:

- **Advertising performance data** — campaign, ad set and ad names, spend,
  impressions, clicks, reach, frequency, and conversion counts.
- **Aggregated audience breakdowns** — age bands, gender, country, placement and
  device. These are statistical totals supplied by Meta. The application does not
  receive, and cannot derive, the identity of any individual within them.
- **Business asset metadata** — ad account, Page, Instagram account and pixel
  identifiers and settings.
- **Organic post metrics** for Instagram and Facebook Pages the operator manages.

## What it does not access

The application does **not** request, collect, receive or store:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses of any consumer.
- Friend lists, private messages, photos, or any personal profile content.
- Payment-card or bank details.
- Any special-category data as defined by GDPR Article 9.

It performs no advertising tracking of its own. It sets no cookies, operates no
pixel, and runs no SDK on any website or mobile application.

## How the data is used

Solely to produce performance reports and budget recommendations for the
operator's own advertising, and to apply changes the operator approves. The data
is never sold, rented, licensed, brokered, or shared with any third party for
their own purposes, and it is not used to train any publicly available
machine-learning model.

## Where it is stored and for how long

Performance data is held in a private database under the operator's sole control,
retained for up to 24 months to allow year-on-year comparison, then deleted.
Access tokens are stored encrypted and are revocable at any time by the granting
Business Portfolio. Nothing is stored on any shared or public host.

## Data deletion

Because the application holds no consumer personal data, there is normally
nothing about an individual to delete. If you believe otherwise, write to
**deepinsightshub@gmail.com** with the detail of your request; it will be
answered within 30 days. See the [data deletion instructions](./data-deletion).

A Business Portfolio administrator may revoke access immediately at
Business Settings → Business assets → Apps, which stops all further collection.
On revocation, stored tokens are destroyed and the associated performance data is
deleted within 30 days on request.

## Your rights

Where GDPR, the UK GDPR, or Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law applies,
you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of personal data concerning
you, and may object to its processing. Write to the contact address above.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised
date. The application's Meta App Review record is updated whenever the scope of
data accessed changes.
